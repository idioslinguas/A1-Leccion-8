Módulo 1

Estructuras de Existencia: There is / There are

En inglés usamos "There is" para singular (hay una sola cosa) y "There are" para plural (hay varias cosas).

1

There is (Singular)

Significa "Hay" (para un solo elemento u objeto)

  • Afirmativo: There is a sofa in the living room.
  • Negativo: There isn't a TV in the bedroom.
  • Pregunta: Is there a fridge in the kitchen?
2

There are (Plural)

Significa "Hay" (para dos o más elementos)

  • Afirmativo: There are four chairs in the dining room.
  • Negativo: There aren't any books on the table.
  • Pregunta: Are there three pillows on the bed?

Vocabulario: Partes de la Casa y Muebles

Módulo 3

Sustantivos Contables e Incontables

Aprende la diferencia entre elementos contables (apples, burgers) e incontables (water, rice, money) y cómo usar Some, Any, Much, Many, How Much, How Many.

Sustantivos Contables (Countable Nouns)

Se pueden contar en unidades de 1, 2, 3...

Tienen forma singular y plural (a banana / three bananas).

Usan How many? para preguntar cantidad: How many apples are there?

Usan many / a few en plurales.

Sustantivos Incontables (Uncountable Nouns)

Líquidos, polvos, masa o conceptos abstractos

No se pueden contar directamente ni tienen plural (*waters no existe).

Usan How much? para preguntar cantidad: How much water is there?

Usan verbos en singular (Water is cold).

A Day at Alex's House (Un día en la casa de Alex)

Párrafo 1 • La Casa

Welcome to Alex’s cozy house in the suburbs! There is a comfortable living room with a large sofa and a bright lamp. In the kitchen, there are four wooden chairs around a round dining table, and there is a big white fridge near the window. Upstairs, there are three small bedrooms and a clean bathroom with a big mirror.

Párrafo 2 • Preguntas Diarias (Wh-)

Every Saturday morning, Alex asks his roommate: "What are we going to cook today?" and "Where is the recipe book?" They always wonder who will visit them for lunch. "When is Sam arriving?" asks Alex. His roommate replies: "He is coming at noon because he loves our homemade pasta!"

Párrafo 3 • Comida (Contables e Incontables)

Before cooking, they check the fridge to see what ingredients they have. There is some milk, a little cheese, and some fresh orange juice, but there isn't any butter. Alex makes a quick list: they need two tomatoes, three apples, and some sugar for the dessert. Rice and pasta are uncountable, so they buy two packets instead.

Párrafo 4 • Preparando el Almuerzo

Now everything is ready on the kitchen table. Alex asks: "How much water do we need for the soup?" and "How many glasses are on the counter?" They prepare a delicious meal together in their warm home. Learning English grammar helps Alex describe his daily life with confidence!
